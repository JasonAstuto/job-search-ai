# ADR 0003: Public alerts only for the MVP

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The initial release should avoid risky automation and respect platform terms of service.

## Decision

The MVP will use public job alerts and user-provided URLs only. Broad scraping and aggressive automation are out of scope for the initial release.

## Consequences

- Lower compliance and legal risk
- Simpler and more reliable ingestion strategy
- Easier initial implementation and validation
